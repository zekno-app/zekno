How it behaves
It changes zekno.app.json and stores secrets on this machine. It contacts no server and no network.
It does everything or nothing: never a service without its secrets.
It never replaces a service, a variable or a secret.
The service is ordinary settings afterwards. zekno settings shows it, and you can change it.
Options
--name <service> another name for the service.
--for <service> who reads the connection secret. Repeat it for several.
--app --server as the other commands.
--yes go ahead without the question.
