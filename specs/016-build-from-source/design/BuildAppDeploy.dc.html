Build from source: the app's deploy screen
Proposed on 2026-11-26, not approved. The full-screen app's deploy screen for a deploy that builds: the panel's two new rows, the build stage, the live line, the summary above the panel, o, a failed build, the header's time and leaving while it builds. Example values only.
Each screen is today's deploy screen (app/screens/deploy.tsx) at 100 by 30, with only the rows of part J of lines.md added; the sentences are those of parts B, D, E, H and I, said as the app says them. The panel is 64 columns in the brand colour, the stage row has the app's gap of three, and the body is 94 columns. Where a window is taller than 30 rows, its caption says so.
